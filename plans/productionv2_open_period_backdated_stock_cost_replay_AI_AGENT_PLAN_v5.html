1. Objective and scope

For every stock-driving transaction in the current OPEN period, permit a future-dated draft, block POST until its Transaction Date reaches the company Business Date, and automatically post a legitimate backdated event while replaying the affected later physical and financial chronology. Preserve CLOSED periods, posted audit evidence, and one atomic transaction. A user must not have to manually roll back later documents merely because an earlier valid document arrived late.

Area Included events and required result
Inventory MR, GR, MI, Scrap, Vendor Return, Transfer, Adjustment, Stock Count; exact slice quantity and WAC/FIFO/Standard value
Procurement PI and Purchase CN settlement, PPV, inventory versus consumed variance, rollback
Production IP, Daily Production, WIP/process handoff, Stock Return, Stock Adjustment, WO Transfer, FG; effective RM→WIP→FG value
Sales DO, direct Invoice shipment, linked-DO Invoice, stock-return CDN, COGS and gross profit; no duplicate issue or COGS
Cross-cutting OPEN-period historical rollback, period close, reconciliation, SQL Server concurrency, failure recovery, performance

Financial-only Sales CDN and linked-DO Invoice keep their existing stock ownership rules. Commercial PI/CN facts remain commercial evidence; replay changes their stock-cost settlement effects, not their invoice amounts. Legacy branches without an ACTIVE compatible V2 epoch retain conservative blockers. Do not change CLOSED-period business chronology or perform a mixed-cost-method online replay.

3. Authority, chronology, and transaction route

  • Company Business Date is the authority for future POST, using StockBusinessTime.NowAsync and the shared future-date guard; compare dates, not server-local wall time. A future draft may be saved only when its selected period is OPEN. POST and rollback must recheck period state under the branch lock. Audit action time remains UTC/current time.
  • Move existing SAVE-time future-date refusals in ProductionFinishedGoodReceiptService, ProductionMaterialIssueService.Draft, ProductionStockReturnService and ProductionStockAdjustmentService to POST-time Business Date validation while retaining their other draft checks. Add the missing explicit future-POST guard in ProductionOutputService.Posting; audit Inventory, Sales and Procurement stock/value entrypoints for the same date contract. The rollout flag is OpenPeriodBackdatedReplayEnabled: it may be ON in Development/Test/UAT for validation but remains OFF in Production until the readiness report is signed.
  • Use one ordered replay event key for physical stock, valuation, dependency traversal and historical rollback: (EffectiveAt, ClassPriority, OriginalPostingSequence, LineIdentity, AttachedSuborder). ClassPriority = 0 for ordinary physical/value-only events and 1 for a stock-count reset; AttachedSuborder = 0 for the base event and then increasing values for its cost deltas/corrections. A restatement inherits its owner's effective time, sequence and line and is applied immediately after that owner, before the next ordinary event. Preserve this original position after restatement; the trigger posting sequence is audit/write ownership only. At equal time a newly posted ordinary event follows existing ordinary events. PI receipt allocation retains its separate proven Business Date → Posting Sequence → Fact ID order.
  • Stock Count is a date-only, end-of-day event. Its canonical logical EffectiveAt is CountDate.Date.AddDays(1).AddTicks(-1) in StockPosting, valuation facts and count-generation metadata, all configured as datetime2(7), with ClassPriority = 1 after every ordinary movement on CountDate, including an ordinary event at that same final tick. Multiple counts on one pile/day follow their original posting sequences. CountDate remains the displayed business date. Keep legacy IvTrxBatch.TrxDtTime/IvTrxHistory.TrxDtTime at CountDate.Date for their existing storage contract; their raw timestamp is not the replay key for a count-owned adjustment. Physical/as-of and financial readers must map those rows through the count generation to the canonical logical key. A later entered same-day ordinary movement precedes the count and triggers replay of its adjustment.
  • Select the existing fast path only when no affected later active event or cost dependency exists. For a true historical dependency, plan a bounded tail from the candidate key through later affected OPEN-period events. Reconstruct strict historical quantities by exact company, branch, item, warehouse, location, lot, status and pile identity. Never use IvBalLoc.TransDate or clamped as-of output as historical authority. Every intermediate slice quantity and final live quantity must be nonnegative.
  • Route into the historical path before old ValidateBalanceStockDate, ValidateNoLaterDayMovementsAsync, RejectBackdatedPoolsAsync and analogous module blockers reject a valid candidate. Keep those checks on the fast/legacy path. The historical writer receives an internal permit created only after the under-lock plan and strict simulation pass; it reuses existing document/detail/history and lock conventions but replaces the old TransDate and live-only chronology decisions with the proven replay result. Do not expose a public or generic skipChronologyChecks switch.
  • Reconstruct each candidate-time pile from current IvBalLoc.StdQty by undoing active later inbound/outbound movements newest to oldest, apply the candidate, then replay the tail oldest to newest. For an outbound event prove both historical source availability and a valid final current balance. Report the first event that would make a slice negative. For a count, recompute its reset delta inside this simulation; the ordinary old current + fixed candidate delta shortcut does not apply. After commit IvBalLoc.TransDate is the latest active physical movement date for that pile, never an earlier backdate; after rollback use the latest remaining active movement or the existing opening-balance rule. Replace date-only TransDate eligibility checks in Inventory source selection, shipment pile discovery, and stock-count candidates with strict as-of logic.
  • Preserve the one-root V2 SQL session context STOCK_LEDGER_V2_POSTING_ID through all guarded writes. A root replay may write corrections whose business owner is another document. StockPostingId identifies the writer; source identity, original history/fact/movement IDs, and normalized replay-effect linkage identify the owner. Refactor owner-specific invariants and readers; never infer business owner solely from root posting ID. No nested V2 posting context without a proven SQL session-context push/restore implementation.
  • Every active chronological query must order a correction by the linked business owner's original posting sequence, not by the later replay root's sequence. Resolve that owner from the normalized effect link or original fact/history/movement lineage. A missing, ambiguous or cyclic owner link is a reconciliation failure. Keep the writer posting ID visible for audit.
  • Before acquiring the branch lock, each POST/rollback entry builds an immutable, read-only impact plan and captures the V2 epoch, branch sequence watermark, root rowversion, affected live rowversions, policy revisions, and plan hash. Begin the existing transaction and coordinator entry; it acquires the branch lock. Under that lock, recheck those tokens, period/freeze guards and idempotency, then lock source header/lines and stock rows in the repository's consistent order. Do not hold a count header/stock row lock before the branch lock. On staleness, roll back and replan outside the lock for at most two retries; then return a retryable stale/busy error with no changes. The count service’s outer transaction follows this same order.
  • Under the single locked root transaction: apply the candidate at its historical position; simulate the full affected physical tail; persist the candidate and append-only physical corrections; replay WAC/FIFO/Standard, procurement, production and sales costs in event order; run all reconciliation gates; call a proposed CompleteReplayInTransactionAsync that verifies every required history has a complete active valuation generation and seals without valuing it again; commit. Any failure rolls back status, balances, facts, correction chains and the root posting together. Add an idempotent coordinator abort/cleanup seam for caller-owned transactions so STOCK_LEDGER_V2_POSTING_ID is cleared after failed replay as well as success; prove the next pooled-connection post succeeds. Keep normal completion unchanged for normal posts.

4. Stock Count: exact behavior and proposed data changes

Draft, count, preview, post

  • SaveAsync/UpdateAsync: permit a future OPEN-period CountDate. Remove IvStockCountLimits.MaxBackdateDays from the date policy. Preserve scope, rowversion, permission and period checks.
  • GenerateAsync, SaveCountsAsync, SetItemCountAsync, PreviewPostAsync and PostAsync: require CountDate <= company Business Date and OPEN period. Generate line SystemQty from strict active historical quantity immediately before the count’s end-of-day key, not current IvBalLoc.StdQty; a new count is provisionally ordered after existing counts of the same pile/day. Include historically eligible piles even when live quantity is zero; apply scope, zero-quantity option and line limit only after historical candidate enumeration. SystemQty and SnapshotUnitPrice remain audit/display evidence.
  • A posted line’s PhysicalQty is the immutable absolute quantity authority. Once a line has a posted generation, SaveCountsAsync and SetItemCountAsync must reject edits even when the header is later ROLLED_BACK; a changed physical observation requires a new sheet. The active adjustment is Round(PhysicalQty - historical pre-count slice quantity, 4): positive means ADJ inbound, negative ADJ outbound. Preview shows this historical pre-count quantity, projected adjustment and downstream effect. Preserve a separate live-quantity column for information. PostedStaleLines compares generated evidence with current historical pre-count quantity, not unrelated later live changes.
  • Every successful post, including all-zero variance, creates one sealed V2 count anchor with source document type IV_STOCK_COUNT, count ID/number, canonical end-of-day EffectiveAt, unique request fingerprint and posting sequence. Use DocumentRevision = 0 on first post and max(sealed count posting DocumentRevision) + 1 for a permitted repost after rollback, under the branch lock; derive RequestId deterministically from tenant, count ID, revision and role, and fingerprint the frozen count quantities/date/cost evidence. A current-date count with no later dependency may use the existing ADJ core inside that root. A historical count uses the replay-specific ADJ write seam because PostInventoryADJCoreAsync currently calls ValidateBalanceStockDate and live-balance checks before replay; do not call it blindly. A zero adjustment creates no ADJ batch/history and is an intentional zero-effect posting, not an orphan. IvStockCountHdr.PostedBatchNo remains the first post’s batch pointer or null, never the active-correction/repost pointer. Generic ADJ edit/rollback must reject count-owned original and correction batches and direct the operator to the count workflow.
  • A late prior movement recomputes the signed adjustment at the original count event, preserving PhysicalQty and its posting sequence. A proposed replay-aware ADJ history writer creates a correction batch/detail and EntryRole = REVERSAL history with ReversesHistoryId for each nonzero prior generation, including a change to zero; it appends EntryRole = ORIGINAL replacement history/valuation only when the new delta is nonzero. The active zero replacement has no adjustment history/batch even though reversal evidence has a correction batch. A direction change is reversal plus opposite-direction replacement. Use the replay root write context and preserve count ownership through generation lineage. Later events start from the frozen physical count and are then replayed.
  • Count rollback removes the active count reset at its original business position, reverses the active generation and replays later events. Reject it atomically if removing the reset would create a negative intermediate slice, cross a CLOSED period, or break a financial dependency. Append a removal generation, set ROLLED_BACK, and retain earlier evidence. RecoverAsync may repair legacy status only after lineage reconciliation; it must not revive an obsolete batch as active. Reposting a rolled-back V2 count uses a new posting revision/anchor and the same frozen physical quantities; a changed count requires a new sheet.

Approved cost basis

For WAC/FIFO, capture a unit cost from the active approved V2 historical valuation state at the count position, together with its branch watermark and deterministic evidence hash. Standard Cost uses the revision effective at the count event. If cost provenance is missing or invalid, reject the triggering replay atomically with the count number and line. Never silently book a zero or stale display cost.
  • The existing InventoryValuationService.AppendReceipt and ResolveFifoReceiptUnitCost reject no-evidence ADJ inbound when on-hand is zero. Add proposed InventoryCostEvidenceTypes.CountFrozenV2 = "COUNT_FROZEN_V2", valid only for a count-owned positive ADJ history linked to an immutable StockCountCostBasis with LEDGER_WAC or LEDGER_FIFO provenance. Extend those valuation paths to validate the basis tenant, item, count line, exact frozen unit cost, and original watermark/hash against the immutable V2 evidence captured at the initial post; do not compare that hash to the newly replayed state. Read the decimal(19,6) unit cost directly from the basis when creating the valuation fact, rather than round-tripping through the ADJ detail's four-decimal display price. An approved zero-cost V2 basis is valid. For an explicit count approval, carry the existing MANUAL_APPROVED or ZERO_COST_APPROVED type, price, reason and approver from the frozen basis into the correction detail/history and use existing validation. 5. Financial and cross-module replay rules

  • A logical IvTrxHistory may own a set of active valuation facts: WAC transfer OUT+IN and FIFO issue/transfer splits. Resolve the complete active generation, not one leaf fact. Exact reversal of every old split/consumption precedes a complete corrected generation. Allocate unique (StockPostingId, PostingLineNo, SplitOrdinal) identities under the root; do not reuse foreign source line numbers. All correction facts use the corrected event’s EffectiveAt, BusinessDate and PeriodKey, while CreatedAtUtc records actual action time.
  • An unchanged physical receipt retains its identity. Cost-only correction is an append-only value delta immediately after the receipt; WAC changes the later average, FIFO adjusts layer accumulated/remaining value and consumed variance, and Standard records its required variance. Do not fabricate a duplicate physical receipt or rewrite sealed facts. Replay must assert one cost method over the affected OPEN-period tail; a corrupt cutover fails closed.
  • WAC algorithm: rewind the active later receipt/issue/value effects by their signed quantity and value, then reapply authoritative receipts, issues at the immediately preceding moving average, and value-only adjustments at their own event keys. Final StockCostState quantity, value and average/current unit cost must match active facts; zero quantity has no unexplained residual value.
  • FIFO algorithm: clone affected live layer state; undo later issue consumptions, receipts and value adjustments newest first without changing audit rows; then allocate forward by receipt effective key and stable origin/layer identity. Reverse every superseded issue split and its consumption rows; append the complete corrected split/consumption generation. Preserve a receipt's history, valuation origin and layer when only its cost changes. Apply its value delta to AccumulatedAdjustment, remaining value and current unit cost; pass the consumed portion into later issues/COGS. Do not rewrite OriginalValue as a shortcut. Open layer quantity/value and every active issue's consumption must exactly reconcile with StockCostState at repository precision.
  • Standard algorithm: use the approved ItemStandardCostRevision effective at each event's business date, include value-only Standard revaluations and Production/Sales-return Standard variances, and leave approved frozen manual costs unchanged. A cost-method cutover inside the OPEN replay tail is unsupported state and blocks the root transaction.
  • PI settlement replay rebuilds active receipt candidates in the proven (BusinessDate, PostingSequence, FactId) order. Include the current transaction’s unsealed late-GR receipt only for its own root posting; exclude every other unsealed receipt. Reverse old active settlement/adjustment generations and append replacements, including PPV and FIFO inventory/consumed split. PI/CN rollback resolves the active generation rather than assuming original roots are unreversed.
  • Introduce one central active resolver for Production movement allocations and pool dependencies: an ordinary row is active only if it has no reversal child; a reversal row is never an active edge. Use it in Shared WIP, pool valuation, transfer/rollback, FG cost trace and dependent detection. Trace corrected edges by movement/business-owner lineage, not equality of correction root posting ID and original movement posting ID. Keep immutable posted Production facts and frozen conversion evidence; append effective-cost restatements. Prove RM→WIP→FG conservation and WO Transfer OUT=IN.
  • Add proposed append-only ProductionCostRestatementFact only for the logical target ProductionBalLotMovementId: Id bigint identity; tenant keys nvarchar(5); ReplayRequestId uniqueidentifier; ReplayStockPostingId bigint tenant-matching FK; ProductionBalLotMovementId bigint FK; EffectiveAt datetime2(7); PriorEffectiveTotalCost, CorrectedEffectiveTotalCost, CorrectedUnitCost, DeltaCost decimal(19,6); ReversesRestatementFactId bigint NULL self-FK unique when non-null; CreatedAtUtc datetime2(7); CreatedBy nvarchar(100). A proposed batch IProductionEffectiveCostResolver selects the active restatement or the immutable original movement cost. Guard restatements against update/delete and index by movement and replay root. Do not duplicate one logical correction across every Production evidence table.
  • Rewind each affected Production pool using effective costs, replay active quantities/allocations/dependencies forward, append restatements only when value changes, and update live pool quantities/value and ProductionPoolValuation together. Detect a dependency cycle and roll back with identifying IDs. IP replacement valuation keeps its material/WO/operation identity and flows to WIP without modifying sealed ProductionMaterialMovement. Daily Production output equals corrected consumed material/WIP cost plus its originally approved frozen conversion cost. Stock Return preserves exact source-issue and slice identity and uses corrected source contribution cost; never restore an old price cache over later activity.
  • Preserve PADJ cost authority by type: CURRENT_PRODUCTION_POOL_COST resolves corrected pool cost at the adjustment key; ORIGINAL_ISSUE_COST resolves corrected source-issue cost; STANDARD_COST uses its event-date approved revision; MANUAL_APPROVED and ZERO_COST_APPROVED stay frozen. PADJ and WO Transfer do not acquire Inventory movements of their own merely because their root writes foreign replay corrections. For WO Transfer restate OUT and IN atomically to identical effective value. For FG restate source WIP, Production effective cost and the existing Inventory receipt value, then propagate FIFO consumption/Standard variance and Sales COGS without a second physical FG receipt.
  • Recalculate downstream DO/direct-Invoice COGS, linked-DO ownership, stock-return CDN allocations and GP from active outbound generations. A return never allocates more active quantity/value than the original sale. Financial-only CDN does not gain stock movement. Rollback reverses the current active generation and replays its historical removal, not stale original facts.
  • Fix StockValuationSnapshotBuilder so FIFO consumption completeness applies to active valued issues only; reversal facts do not require issue consumption rows. Month-end signed snapshots include original, reversal and replacement facts in their corrected business periods. Never move a CLOSED-period correction into the current month.
